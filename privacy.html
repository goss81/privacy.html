# Privacy Policy — Mijn Discord-bot **Last updated:** October 8, 2026 ## 1. Information We Process This bot may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, usernames, role information, voice channel activity, and messages needed for moderation and anti-spam features. ## 2. How We Use Information Information is processed to provide the bot's features, manage temporary voice channels, assign roles, detect potential spam, apply moderation actions, and maintain server logs. ## 3. Data Storage and Sharing The bot administrator should document any information stored by the bot, how long it is retained, and whether it is shared with any service providers. Information is not intentionally sold. ## 4. Data Retention and Deletion Users may contact the bot administrator to ask questions about their data or request deletion. Requests will be handled in accordance with applicable law. ## 5. Security Reasonable measures should be taken to protect bot credentials and any information the bot processes. ## 6. Contact For privacy questions or data requests, contact the bot administrator through the Discord server where the bot is used.
